Common questions

How does renting through CrestHaven work?

Choose a property, book a viewing and submit a rental application. We check references, agree the lease terms with the owner and arrange the move-in date.

Can I make an offer on a property online?

Yes. Open the property page, choose Make an offer and share your proposed price. An advisor will contact you to discuss the next steps.

How do I sell or list my property?

Use the Sell Property page to submit your listing in four steps. Our team reviews every listing before it goes live.

Do you help with architects and interior designers?

Yes. Use the Find an Architect page to share your plot, budget and style, and we will match you with a professional.
